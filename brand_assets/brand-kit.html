07 · Logo Usage Rules
Do
  • Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the "P" in PACIFIC on all sides
  • Use the white-background logo on light/warm backgrounds
  • Use the dark-background logo on dark or coloured backgrounds
  • Maintain the original aspect ratio at all times
  • Minimum display size: approximately 100px (digital) / 25mm (print)
  • Use high-resolution PNG or vector source files
Don't
  • Never alter, recolor, or apply effects to the logo
  • Never stretch or distort the logo proportions
  • Never place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without overlay
  • Never rotate or flip the logo in any direction
  • Never recreate or approximate the logo in alternate fonts
  • Never use the logo at a size that makes it illegible
